2. Title Tag Analysis

What to check:

Does the homepage have a title tag?
Is the title descriptive and relevant to the page?
Does it accurately represent the website?
Is it unique compared with other important pages?

Finding: Record the actual title after inspecting the page source or browser SEO tools.

Recommendation: Write a unique, descriptive title for each important page, placing the main topic near the beginning where appropriate.

3. Meta Description Analysis

What to check:

Is a meta description present?
Does it describe the page accurately?
Is it unique for each important page?
Does it communicate a clear reason to visit?

Finding: Verify the homepage and selected product page descriptions.

Recommendation: Write helpful, page-specific descriptions that accurately summarize the content and encourage relevant users to click.

4. Heading Hierarchy

What to check:

Is the main page topic clearly represented by an H1?
Are H2 and H3 headings used logically?
Do headings describe the content that follows?
Are headings used for structure rather than only visual styling?

Finding: Inspect the homepage and one product or category page.

Recommendation: Organize content into a clear hierarchy with descriptive headings and avoid unnecessary repetition.

5. Image Alt Attributes

What to check:

Do meaningful product images have appropriate alt text?
Does the alt text describe the image accurately?
Are decorative images handled appropriately?
Is keyword stuffing avoided?

Finding: Inspect at least five representative images and record examples.

Recommendation: Add concise, useful alt text to informative images. Use empty alt attributes for purely decorative images where appropriate.

6. URL Structure

What to check:

Are URLs readable and descriptive?
Do they reflect the page's content?
Are unnecessary parameters or confusing paths present?
Are canonical URLs and redirects configured correctly?

Finding: Compare the homepage, one category page, and one product page.

Recommendation: Use stable, descriptive URLs and check canonicalization and redirects before proposing changes.

7. Internal Linking

What to check:

Can users navigate between categories and products?
Are important pages linked from relevant pages?
Are anchor texts descriptive?
Are any important pages difficult to discover?

Recommendation: Improve relevant internal links and ensure important pages are accessible through logical navigation.

8. Mobile Usability

What to check:

Does the layout adapt to smaller screens?
Is text readable without zooming?
Are buttons and navigation easy to use?
Do product images and page elements fit the screen?

Recommendation: Test representative pages on mobile and resolve any observed usability issues.
